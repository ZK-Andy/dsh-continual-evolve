<h3 id="cn-v0.9.0">新功能（通用自动进化收紧）</h3>

- 通用自动进化三方向收紧：review 默认 decline 单次交互（同一模式 ≥2 次重复证据才放行）、planner 禁单次交互 local prompt/skill/subagent（无重复证据返回空 edits）、fate turn 路径永不弹窗（只 silent 归档，治理动作指到 `/evolve wrapup`，仅 `goal_blocked` 保留 consult）(@zhangkun) <!-- 485736f -->

<h3 id="en-v0.9.0">Features (general auto-evolution tightening)</h3>

- General auto-evolution tightened on three fronts: review declines single-interaction evidence by default (same pattern needs ≥2 repeated sightings), the planner emits no local prompt/skill/subagent edits without repeated evidence, and the fate turn path never pops up (silent archive only, governance deferred to `/evolve wrapup`, consult kept for `goal_blocked` only) (@zhangkun) <!-- 485736f -->

### Bug Fixes

- Hash-fallback ids for non-ASCII-only titles (Chinese titles always get an id) (@zhangkun) <!-- ee32d89 -->
- Actionable-specificity rule for the extractor, pinned with vague-vs-sharp examples (@zhangkun) <!-- ee673a0 -->

### Chores

- Doc gates migrated to TS with a per-file coverage waterline: four gates rewritten as `scripts/verify-*.ts` (aggregated via `tsx`), per-file thresholds in `vitest.config.ts` (branches 75→80→83), plus a `coverage-gaps.ts` locator (`pnpm coverage:gaps`) (@zhangkun) <!-- c39b260 bc7444b -->
- Twelve coverage rounds: 783→1032 tests (54 files), statements 92.52→98.9, branches 82.27→92.6, functions 95.85→99.51; 14 files at triple-100; bilingual README coverage badges (@zhangkun) <!-- bb18a57 05a0281 e429eba b2dbcfc 0a341fd fd9fb4a 9b8ab2c 81fdef5 7d00882 7844324 34e86a2 -->
