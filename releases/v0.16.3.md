<h3 id="en-v0.16.3">v0.16.3 — In-card settings plus layer remediation</h3>

The review-settings surface lands inside the bundle page: two cards under one entry (global settings plus the memory partition), with GET reading the live volatile snapshot and POST merging through `configEditor.edit`. The v2 layer remediation lands in full: facets rolled back to one `MemoryStore` interface, reverse dependencies killed, 17 root files homed in `services/`, the assembly root thinned to 230 lines, the card as a page, and the R1–R4 semantic gate in the gate chain; coverage backfilled to 512 tests with CI green.

### Features

- In-card review settings (@ZK-Andy) <!-- 877945f -->: twin cards on the bundle page; GET serves the volatile snapshot, POST writes back to the profile patch; `auto:false` suppresses the auto page.

### Bug Fixes

- Coverage gate repaired (@ZK-Andy) <!-- 39110a0 -->: 21 new behavior tests for the assembly, card mount, and settings routes (512 green); the CI coverage gate is green again (it was already red at v0.16.2).
